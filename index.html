Upload a plate reader CSV or Excel file exported from Welly or your plate reader software.

Browser-based microbial growth curve analysis for 96- and 384-well plates. Upload your absorbance time series, assign sample names via plate map, and get interactive growth curves, max growth rates, and AUC values — all computed locally. No data leaves your machine. Built for the Welly platform at Cerillo.

Absorbance file format
  • CSV or Excel (.xlsx) from your plate reader
  • First column: Duration (Hours) or Time
  • Remaining columns: one per well (e.g. A1, A2 … H12)
  • 96-well: 97 columns total · 384-well: 385 columns total
  • Welly exports are supported natively
Plate layout map format
  • CSV with row letters in column 1 (A–H or A–P)
  • Column numbers in the header row (1–12 or 1–24)
  • Cell values = sample names (e.g. WT, Mutant1,c1)
  • Leave cells blank to exclude wells from analysis
  • Replicate wells with the same name are grouped automatically
How it works
1
Upload your absorbance time series CSV
2
Map wells — upload a plate layout CSV or fill in the grid manually
3
Review the max OD heatmap to catch outlier wells
4
Analyze — growth curves, max growth rate (OD/hr), and AUC (OD·h) with error bars across replicates
Metrics computed
  • Growth curve — mean OD ± SD over time per sample
  • Max growth rate — peak ΔOD/Δt (OD/hr) per replicate, then averaged
  • Area under curve — trapezoidal integration (OD·h) per replicate
  • Download: HTML report, summary CSV, or PNG charts
